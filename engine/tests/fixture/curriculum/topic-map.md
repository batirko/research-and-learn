# Base

*What is going on inside the hive?*

How a colony lives through a year, so the reader knows what they're looking at.

## The colony

### B01 How a colony works
Critical | S | C1 | read first | pilot

**Why:** every decision at the hive is a bet on what the colony does next.

**Starting questions:** Who does which work? How long does each kind of bee take to emerge? What happens when the queen is lost?

**Visual:** likely. The three development times on one axis.

### B02 The beekeeping year
High | S | C1 | after B01

**Why:** the season sets what a colony needs from the beekeeper each month.

**Starting questions:** When does a colony grow, swarm, store and shrink? What does the beekeeper do in each stretch?

**Seeds:** the association's calendar, a county agricultural extension's monthly guide.

# Practical

*What do I do at the hive, and when?*

What a beekeeper does in the first season, and how to tell it worked.

## At the hive

### P01 Inspecting a hive
Critical | S | C2 | after B01 | pilot

**Why:** an inspection is the only way to see what the colony is doing.

**Starting questions:** What do you look for, in what order? How long can a hive stay open? What do you write down?

### P02 Swarm control
High | M | C2 | after P01

**Why:** a swarm in the first season halves the colony and its honey.

**Starting questions:** What signs come before a swarm? Which method suits a beekeeper with two hives?

**Visual:** maybe. The days between sealed queen cells and a swarm.

# Modern

*What do today's monitors change?*

What scales, sensors and apps add to looking inside the hive.

## Monitors

### M01 Hive monitors: scales, sensors and apps
Medium | S | C3 | after B01 | updates B01, P01 | facts age fast | pilot

**Why:** monitors promise fewer inspections, and the reader has to decide whether two hives justify one.

**Starting questions:** What can a scale tell that an inspection can't? What can't it tell?
