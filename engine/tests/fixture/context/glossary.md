# Glossary

The one word to use for each concept. A word in the "Don't use" column is banned in every topic. A word in italics there is banned only in this row's sense.

## The colony

| Use | Meaning | Don't use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **brood** | Eggs, larvae and pupae, together. B01 is the home. | baby bees |
| **queen** | The one female in a colony that lays eggs. | queen bee |
| **forager** | A worker in her last weeks, who collects nectar, pollen and water. Added from B01 (D2). | field bee |
| **sealed brood** | Larvae in cells the workers have capped with wax. | *capped* |
