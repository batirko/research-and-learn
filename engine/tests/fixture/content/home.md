# First season with bees

This guide prepares you for your first season with two hives. It explains how a colony works, how to look inside one, and what today's monitors add. You read each topic's explanation in full, then pick from a few ranked materials for depth.

It is also the engine's test guide. Its subject is real, its case is invented, and nobody checked its facts.

## How the ranks work

Every topic, section and material has a rank. A critical topic is one you can't start the season without. A critical material is one to read in full, because it gives something this guide can't.

## Private facts

Facts from private sources sit in blocks marked Private, closed until you open them. Each fact carries a tag that names its source.
