# The reader

Who reads the guide, and what that means for how you write. Every topic worker reads this note. A topic's "Why it matters" cites the list in "What the goal asks of the reader". A bridge cites an item in "What they have done".

> **To fill this file.** The set-up skill fills it from the interview, and the scaffold adds what its research finds. Write about the reader in the third person, by first name. Fill each section, and delete each "To fill" note once its section holds the answer. Mark anything the reader said in confidence with a private tag, and keep it out of the topics unless it sits in a private block.

## Who reads

> **To fill:** one short paragraph. The reader's first name, which also goes in `guide.reader` in `guide.toml`. What they're preparing for, and by when. Whether they're the owner.

## What they know, and what they don't

> **To fill:** what the reader has heard of and what they've studied, in the subject and next to it. Name the gaps.

Write from zero. Assume the reader has heard of the subject and hasn't studied it. A strong background next to the subject doesn't excuse a skipped explanation.

## What they have done

> **To fill:** a list of experiences a topic can bridge to, one line each, concrete enough to name in a sentence. For example: "Has kept chickens for six years: daily checks, a fox-proof run, and records of every egg." Leave out anything the reader wouldn't want in the guide.

## How they read

> **To fill:** the language they read in, and whether it's their first. Where and when they'll read: a laptop, a phone, a commute. What they do with materials. By default, they read the guide's text and critical materials in full, skim high and medium materials, and open context ones when needed.

## What they want from the prose

> **To fill:** the reader's own words on how they like to be taught, quoted where they said it. For example, whether they want the reasoning before a recommendation, or a worked example first.

## What the goal asks of the reader

> **To fill:** the abilities from the goal in `docs/request.md`, as a numbered list. Add anything the case will ask of the reader that the scaffold's research found, tagged with its source.

## Time

> **To fill:** the date, the hours a day, and the reading days left at kickoff, from `docs/request.md`. Add anything that changes the pace, such as a week without reading time.
