# Research

One dossier per topic: the topic's research record. A dossier holds every source the topic worker considered, and what it decided about each. The next session then doesn't search again for what was already weighed. The portal never shows a dossier.

## Name

`research/<id>-<slug>.md`, with the same ID and slug as the topic's folder, such as `research/p01-inspecting-a-hive.md`. The topic worker creates it when it starts and keeps it current as it works, so a stop loses nothing.

## What a dossier holds

1. **Searches:** what you searched for, where, and on what date. Say where you found nothing, too.
2. **Candidates:** every source you opened, each with a verdict. Kept: its use, evidence or material, and its rank. Dropped: the reason, in one line.
3. **Evidence:** for each outside claim in the topic, the sentence in the source that it rests on, quoted, with the link and the date you checked it. A reviewer who can't reach the web checks the claim against this quote.
4. **Pages about the case:** what a public page said and when, for any page that might change or vanish.
5. **Open ends:** what you couldn't settle, and the leads you didn't follow.

Quote only what the claim rests on, one or two sentences. A dossier is a record, not a copy of the source.
