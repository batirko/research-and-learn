# Brief: context worker, the association's public pages

## Goal

Check and extend what is known about the Hillside association, from its public pages:

- Whether the association lends an extractor, and how a member books it (OQ-02).
- Whether every link in `context/association.md` still opens, and still says what the note says it does.

## Why

B02, P02 and the open-questions page rest on what the association does. The scaffold wrote `context/association.md` from the handbook and two public pages, and three of its claims carry `[unverified]`. A wrong fact here spreads into every topic that cites it. B02 and P02 are being written now, so a change you find goes into their fix round.

## Model and budget

You run on the strongest model. Your budget is about 100,000 tokens.

Before you start, fetch the association's home page. If the fetch fails, stop and say so in your handoff.

## Read before you start

1. `AGENTS.md`
2. The run picture: `orchestration/briefs/run-02/picture.md`.
3. `context/README.md`: the provenance tags, and how a note keeps evidence apart from interpretation.
4. `context/association.md`
5. `context/open-questions.md` and `context/never-publish.md`.
6. The handbook in `context/sources/`, by search.

## Fixed

- Public sources only. The members' area needs a login Sam doesn't have yet, so stay out of it.
- Every fact gets a provenance tag, and a public fact gets its link.
- Open the page. A search snippet doesn't confirm anything.
- Sources are frozen. To keep what a page says today, add a new dated file to `context/sources/`, and its row to the list in `context/sources/README.md`. Never change an old file.
- Record what a page says. Label what you make of it, apart.
- When a page contradicts the note, the page wins. Correct the note, and say so in your handoff.
- Nothing on the never-publish list goes into a note.

## Yours to decide

Where to look, and in what order. Useful places include the association's own pages, its newsletter archive and its posts about the teaching apiary.

## What you own

- `context/association.md`
- New files named `context/sources/{name}-2026-10-06.md`, and their rows in `context/sources/README.md`.
- `orchestration/handoffs/2026-10-06-context-association.md`.

Propose changes to OQ-02 in your handoff: the orchestrator owns the open questions. Don't run git or the build.

## Hand back

The updated note, with the date of each change, and the handoff. The handoff says what changed, and which topics it affects. If no public page says how to book the extractor, say so. OQ-02 then stays open for the secretary, and that's a result.

Finish with a short summary for someone who wasn't watching:

- what changed;
- what stayed unknown;
- the tokens you think you used.
